What is Hubrave?

Creative Technology & Digital Solutions

Hubrave is a creative technology agency focused on helping startups, businesses, and organizations grow in the digital space. We build modern websites, craft intuitive user experiences, and develop tailored digital solutions that strengthen a brand's online presence.

Our approach combines visual aesthetics with rigorous technical standards to build products that are beautiful, accessible, and results-driven.

Avni's Role: Co-founder & UX/Tech Lead

  • Creative & Design Direction: Constructing design tokens, high-fidelity prototypes, and UI specifications in Figma to establish modern brand identities.
  • Frontend Architecture: Developing responsive React & Next.js websites, ensuring clean CSS layouts, semantic HTML, and fluid animation timings.
  • Operations & Delivery: Leading design sprints, collaborating with clients, and overseeing project releases from initial concept to deployment.
